Global Adult Obesity Rates 2023

Figure 1: This choropleth map that shows the share of adults with BMI ≥ 30 in 2023. Unfortunately, not all countries like Antartica are included in this analysis due to unavaliability. High obesity rates are strongly visible in North America (United States, Mexico) and parts of the Middle East & North Africa (e.g., Saudi Arabia, Egypt). These regions appear in the darker orange-brown shades, indicating obesity rates above 30-40%.
