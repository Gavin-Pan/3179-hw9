The Scarcity of Obesity

Global Adult Obesity Rates 2023

This map that shows the share of adults with BMI ≥ 30 in 2023. Unfortunately, not all countries like Antartica are included in this analysis due to unavaliability of data. High obesity rates are strongly visible in North America (United States, Mexico) and parts of the Middle East & North Africa (e.g., Saudi Arabia, Egypt). These regions appear in the darker orange-brown shades, indicating obesity rates above 30-40%.

Obesity Rate vs Sugar Consumption

Sugar can be an amending tool to help with lowering an individual's obesity. With the limied data avalaible in terms of countries, it's suggested that for the most part, couunties that engange in a lower average sugar consumption (kg per capita) that their obesity rates are lower. However, this is a weak association and several high-obesity countries appear despite relatively modest sugar intake. This suggests sugar alone does not explain cross-country obesity differences—other factors like physical activity.These values are country averages across available years; they show correlation, not causation.
